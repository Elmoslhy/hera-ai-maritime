<div class="ci" data-i="4"><span>5 of 6</span><h3>So does its own radar.</h3><p>Two satellites hear its navigation radar on X-band and measure where the signal comes from.</p></div>
<div class="ci" data-i="5"><span>6 of 6</span><h3>Found, and named.</h3><p>HERA AI fuses heat and radio into one position at 97% confidence, and matches the ship to its IMO number.</p></div>
